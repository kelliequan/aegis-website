Escrow & disputes

Your money is safe until you are.

AEGIS holds the payment while the deal happens, and a fair process steps in if anything goes wrong.

Escrow

How the money moves

Escrow means a trusted third party holds the payment until both sides have done what they promised. Here, that third party is AEGIS.

1 · AWAITING PAYMENT

The deal starts

The tenant starts a deal and the property is reserved. The tenant is shown how to pay.

2 · PAYMENT SUBMITTED

The tenant pays and submits proof

The payment goes to AEGIS. The tenant submits the payment in the app so AEGIS can verify it.

3 · FUNDED

Held safely in escrow

AEGIS confirms the money has arrived. Everyone in the deal is notified. The owner now knows the tenant is serious and the tenant knows the owner cannot touch the money yet.

4 · RELEASED

The tenant confirms move-in

When the tenant has the keys and everything is as agreed, they confirm in the app. This releases the payment for payout.

5 · COMPLETED

The owner is paid out

AEGIS pays the owner into their registered payout account and marks the deal Completed.

Everyone is told, every time. At each stage the tenant, the agent or landlord, and AEGIS all receive the same notification, so nobody has to chase for updates.
Disputes

When a deal does not go to plan

Either side can raise a dispute once the payment is funded and before move-in is confirmed. A written reason is required, so the issue is clear from the start.

1. Raise it

The tenant or the owner or agent opens a dispute and explains what went wrong. The deal is frozen and the money stays held.

2. AEGIS reviews

The AEGIS team looks at the deal history, the messages and any evidence from both sides.

3. A decision

AEGIS either releases the money to the owner, or refunds it to the tenant, depending on what the evidence shows.

If the owner wins

The payment is released and the deal can complete. The tenant's trust score takes a penalty for the lost dispute.

If the tenant wins

The money is refunded to the tenant and the property becomes available again. The owner's trust score takes a penalty for the lost dispute.

Cancelling

Changing your mind, safely

Before payment is confirmed, a deal can be cancelled by the tenant, the owner or AEGIS. The property becomes available again straight away. If AEGIS cannot confirm a payment, the deal returns to awaiting payment so the tenant can resubmit.

What this protects you from

  • Paying someone who then disappears.
  • Handing over keys before payment is confirmed.
  • The same property being sold or rented twice.
  • Arguments with no record of what happened.
