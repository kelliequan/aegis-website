For landlords

Reach serious tenants. Get paid securely.

List your property once, deal with verified people and let AEGIS handle the money safely.

Why landlords use AEGIS

Less risk, less chasing

  • List with confidence. Your property earns the Aegis Verified mark after AEGIS reviews it.
  • Know the money is real. Tenant payments are confirmed in escrow before you hand over keys.
  • No double-booking. A property in a deal is marked reserved automatically and opens again if the deal is cancelled.
  • Get paid out by AEGIS into your registered payout account when the deal completes.
  • Stay informed. You receive an alert at every stage of the deal.
  • A fair process. If a disagreement happens, AEGIS reviews the evidence and decides.

Working through an agent?

That is fine. You can list through a verified agent and still see the deal progress. Check the agent's tier, deals closed and reviews first, just as a tenant would.

Build your own reputation

Landlords have a trust score too. Verified identity, completed deals and good ratings all raise it, which makes serious tenants more comfortable choosing your property.

Getting paid: you register the bank account for payouts in your profile. AEGIS only pays out to the account registered on your verified profile.
