Verification

Know exactly who you are dealing with.

AEGIS checks real identities and reviews listings, so a Verified badge actually means something.

People

Identity verification

Anyone who wants to list or close deals as an owner or agent proves who they are. It takes a few minutes in the app.

  • Government ID. You submit your National Identification Number (NIN) and photos of your government ID.
  • Live selfie. A selfie liveness check confirms the person holding the ID is really you.
  • Identity check and review. The details are checked through an identity verification provider and then reviewed by the AEGIS team.
  • Verified badge. Once approved, your profile shows as Verified and your trust score gets a boost.

Your data stays private

  • Only the last four digits of your NIN are kept.
  • ID photos and selfies are stored privately and are never shown to other users.
  • Other users only see your verification status, never your documents.

Read the full detail in our privacy policy.

What a listing goes through

SubmittedAEGIS reviewAegis Verified

Listings that fail review are rejected. A verified listing that later looks suspicious can be flagged for review, hidden from verified results or delisted.

Properties

Listing verification

Photos and details are reviewed by the AEGIS team before a listing earns the Aegis Verified mark.

  • Owners and agents add property details, photos, videos and supporting evidence.
  • AEGIS approves, rejects or flags listings after reviewing them.
  • Reported listings are looked into, and removed if they are not genuine.
  • Once a property is in a deal, it is marked reserved so it cannot be double-booked.
Verification can be removed. If a verified user is later found to have broken the rules, AEGIS can revoke their verification. Their trust score drops accordingly and the badge disappears from their profile.
