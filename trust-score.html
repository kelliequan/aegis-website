Trust score

Trust is earned, and everyone can see it.

Every AEGIS profile carries a score out of 100, a tier badge, a count of deals closed and real reviews.

Tier badges

Five tiers, one glance.

The badge tells you at a glance how established someone is on AEGIS.

How it is calculated

What raises and lowers your score

The rules are simple and the same for everyone. Some categories have a ceiling, so a score cannot be inflated by repeating one easy action.

Verified identityGovernment ID and selfie approved by AEGIS+15
Complete your profileA one-time boost for a finished profile+5
Each completed dealFor tenants, landlords and agents alike, up to a ceiling+0.2
A good rating (4 or 5 stars)When the other party rates you after a deal, up to a ceiling+0.3
Time on AEGISFor every month your account has been active, up to a ceiling+1
Lose a disputeWhen AEGIS decides a dispute against you−0.5
A report upheld against youWhen AEGIS finds a report against you to be true−0.5
Backing out of a dealWalking away after committing−5
Verification revokedIf AEGIS removes your Verified status−15

Scores stay between 0 and 100. Point values may be fine-tuned over time as AEGIS grows.

Deals closed

Every profile shows how many deals a person has successfully closed. It is a record that is hard to fake and easy to understand.

Ratings & reviews

After a completed deal, both sides can rate each other from 1 to 5 stars with a short comment. Averages and recent reviews appear on profiles.

Fair by design

Small steps per deal mean trust builds with consistent, honest dealing, not with a quick win.
