Privacy Policy

AEGIS PROP TECH · Effective 15 October 2026

AEGIS is a trust and verification platform for property rentals and sales in Nigeria. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who we are

AEGIS PROP TECH ("AEGIS", "we", "us") operates the AEGIS mobile app and related services. For any privacy question you can reach us at admin@aegisproptech.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, email address, role (tenant, agent or landlord), profile photo and business name if you add one.
  • Identity verification: your National Identification Number (NIN), photos of your government ID, and a selfie used for a liveness check. We keep only the last four digits of your NIN. ID photos and selfies are stored privately and are visible only to AEGIS verification staff.
  • Listing content: property details, photos, videos, the evidence you upload for a listing, and the fees you declare.
  • Activity in the app: applications, saved properties, messages and attachments, reports you file, and transaction records for payments held through AEGIS escrow.
  • Payout details: bank account details that an owner provides so that AEGIS can pay out a completed transaction.
  • Trust information: your verification status, trust score, number of completed deals, and ratings and reviews you give or receive.
  • Device information: a notification token, if you allow push notifications, so that we can send you alerts about your account and deals.

3. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account, and to verify that people on AEGIS are who they say they are.
  • To show listings, connect tenants with owners and agents, and let you message each other.
  • To hold and release payments safely through escrow, and to handle disputes and reports.
  • To calculate and display trust scores, deal counts and reviews, so that others can judge who to deal with.
  • To send service messages and notifications about your account, applications and transactions.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to meet our legal obligations.

4. Our legal basis

We process your data to perform our agreement with you (running your account and your deals), because you have given consent (for example, for identity verification and push notifications), to comply with the law, and for our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe and trustworthy. You can withdraw consent at any time, although some features may then stop working.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your name, photo, role, business name, verification status, trust score, deals closed and reviews appear on your public profile and on your listings. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Your ID documents and payout details are never shown to other users.
  • Service providers that help us run AEGIS, including cloud database and file storage, an identity verification provider, and a push notification provider. They may process data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities where the law requires it, or where needed to protect people from fraud or harm.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. How we protect your data

We use access controls, encrypted connections and private storage for sensitive documents, and we limit staff access to what they need. No system is perfectly secure, so please also protect your login details.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active and for as long as needed to run the service, resolve disputes and meet legal and accounting duties. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete Account) and your personal data will be removed, except records we must keep by law or to settle a dispute.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict certain processing, receive a copy of your data, and withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, email admin@aegisproptech.com. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

AEGIS is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us and we will remove the account.

10. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers store or process data outside Nigeria. Where that happens, we take steps to make sure your data stays protected as the NDPA requires.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as AEGIS grows. When we make an important change we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
