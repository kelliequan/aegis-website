FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about how AEGIS works. Cannot find yours? Get in touch.

What is AEGIS?

AEGIS is a trust and verification platform for property rentals and sales in Nigeria. We verify the people and listings, hold payments safely in escrow and resolve disputes, so deals can be trusted.

When does the app launch?

The AEGIS app is launching soon. The Launch app and store buttons on this site will switch on the moment it is live. Follow @aegisproptech on X for launch news.

Which phones will it work on?

Android first. An iPhone version is planned after that.

Is AEGIS free to use?

Pricing and any fees will be shared clearly at launch, and shown before you commit to anything.

How does escrow protect me?

When you start a deal, you pay AEGIS rather than the owner. AEGIS holds the money until you confirm move-in. Only then is it paid out to the owner.

What if the owner or agent does not hand over the keys?

Do not confirm move-in. Raise a dispute in the app with a written reason. AEGIS reviews the evidence and can refund your money.

What if the tenant tries to cheat me?

The money is confirmed in escrow before you hand over keys. If a dispute is raised, AEGIS decides it using the deal history and evidence from both sides.

Who decides disputes?

The AEGIS team reviews the deal history, the messages and any evidence provided, then decides whether to release the money to the owner or refund the tenant.

What does the Verified badge mean?

The person submitted a government ID and a live selfie, and AEGIS reviewed and approved them. It does not mean AEGIS guarantees their behaviour, which is why trust scores and reviews matter too.

Is my ID safe?

ID photos and selfies are stored privately and are never shown to other users. We only keep the last four digits of your NIN. See our privacy policy for details.

What is the trust score?

A number out of 100 that rises with verified identity, completed deals, good ratings and time on the platform, and falls with lost disputes and upheld reports. It is shown with a tier badge on every profile.

What does deals closed mean?

The number of deals a person has successfully completed through AEGIS. It is shown on their profile.

Can I rate the other person?

Yes. After a deal is completed, both sides can leave a 1 to 5 star rating and a short comment.

How do owners get paid?

When a deal completes, AEGIS pays the owner into the payout account registered on their profile.

A listing looks suspicious. What do I do?

Report it from the app. AEGIS can flag, hide or remove it. Never pay outside the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile and choose Delete Account. Some records may be kept where the law requires or to settle a dispute. See the privacy policy.

How do I contact AEGIS?

Email admin@aegisproptech.com or message @aegisproptech on X.
