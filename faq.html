FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about AEGIS. Cannot find yours? Get in touch.

What is AEGIS?

AEGIS is a trust and verification platform for property in Nigeria. We verify people and listings and help keep payments safe, so deals can be trusted.

When does the app launch?

The AEGIS app is launching soon. Follow @aegisproptech on X to hear the moment it goes live.

Which phones will it work on?

Android first. An iPhone version is planned after that.

Is AEGIS free to use?

Pricing and any fees will be shared clearly at launch.

How does AEGIS keep my money safe?

Payments for a deal are held securely by AEGIS until the deal is confirmed.

What does Verified mean?

It means AEGIS has checked and approved that person or listing. It is one of several signals to help you decide who to deal with.

Is my personal information safe?

We take privacy seriously. Your documents are stored privately and never shown to other users. See our privacy policy for details.

A listing looks suspicious. What do I do?

Report it from the app, or email us. Never pay outside the app.

How do I contact AEGIS?

Email admin@aegisproptech.com or message @aegisproptech on X.
