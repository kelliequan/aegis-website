The problem

Renting and buying in Nigeria should not feel like a gamble.

Too many people lose money, time and peace of mind because there is no way to tell who and what is real.

Fake listings

Photos copied from other sites, properties that do not exist, or that are already taken.

Unverified agents

Anyone can claim to be an agent or an owner. There is rarely any proof.

Money sent blindly

Rent and fees are paid straight to strangers, with no protection if things go wrong.

Nobody to judge

When a deal goes bad, there is no neutral party and no track record to learn from.

The solution

AEGIS puts proof behind every deal.

Five protections, working together, from the first search to the final handover of keys.

Verified people

Users prove who they are with a government ID and a live selfie. AEGIS reviews every submission before a Verified badge is granted.

Verified listings

Listings are reviewed by AEGIS before they carry the Aegis Verified mark. Suspicious ones can be flagged or removed.

Escrow payments

The tenant's payment is held safely by AEGIS and is only released once move-in is confirmed.

Dispute resolution

If something goes wrong, either side can raise a dispute. AEGIS reviews the evidence and decides the outcome.

Visible trust

Every profile shows a trust score, a tier badge, deals closed and real reviews, so reputation is earned and visible.

Everyone stays informed

Tenant, agent or landlord and AEGIS all get the same alert at each step of the deal. No one is left guessing.

How it works

From search to keys, in four steps.

TENANT

Find a verified listing

Browse properties and check who is behind them: their verification status, trust tier, deals closed and reviews.

TENANT

Start a deal and pay into escrow

The property is reserved for you. Your payment goes to AEGIS, not to a stranger, and is confirmed by AEGIS.

TENANT

Move in and confirm

Inspect, collect your keys and confirm the agreement. If something is wrong, raise a dispute before you confirm.

OWNER

Get paid and build trust

AEGIS pays out the owner. Both sides earn trust, deals closed and the chance to leave a rating.
