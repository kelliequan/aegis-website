3Verification, badges and trust scores

To earn a badge you may be asked for your National Identification Number (NIN), photos of a government ID (front and back) and a selfie for a liveness check. Agencies are also asked for their business name, CAC registration number, office address, CAC certificate and the owner's ID. We may use identity verification providers to carry out these checks.

Badges read "AEGIS Verified Tenant", "AEGIS Verified Agent", "AEGIS Verified Agency" or "AEGIS Verified Landlord". They show that AEGIS reviewed the documents and details you gave and found them to match at the time of the check. The person is not an employee or representative of AEGIS.

A badge is not a guarantee. It is not an endorsement of anyone's honesty or of a property's title, and it does not protect against every kind of fraud. Always inspect the property, ask for documents and use your own judgment.

Trust score: every profile shows a score from 0 to 100, with tiers New, Trusted and Highly Trusted, plus the number of deals closed and reviews. The score goes up with ID verification and successfully completed deals, and goes down with upheld reports and lost disputes. We may adjust the rules to keep the score fair. If you think your score is wrong, email support@aegisproptech.com and a person will review it.

We may remove a badge if information turns out to be false or out of date, or if the rules are broken.

4Listings and viewings
  • Only landlords, agents and agencies who have the right or authority to list a property may post it. Posting may need an active subscription.
  • Every listing needs one walkthrough video of the whole property and photos of its different areas. Show the real property and keep the listing up to date. For shortlets, one video is required and you can add as many photos as you like.
  • State the rent or price honestly, and list every extra fee clearly, for example caution, agreement and service charge. An agent fee may be listed only on agent and agency listings, never on a landlord's own listing.
  • Mark a property as rented or unavailable as soon as it is no longer available.
  • We may hide or remove a listing that is misleading, duplicated, unsafe or against these Terms.

Each property and each profile has a share link. Anyone who has the link can see the public parts of that page.

View a property in person, or through someone you trust, before you pay anything.

5Messaging and voice calls

  • Voice calls run inside the app. After a call, a line appears in the chat showing its length, or whether it was missed or declined.
  • Never share your login code or password with anyone. AEGIS staff will never ask you for your login code.
6Escrow, fees and payouts

Where escrow is available on a deal, the tenant's payment is held under the AEGIS escrow arrangement instead of going straight to the other party. A typical deal works like this:

  1. 1You agree the deal in the app.
  2. 2The tenant pays through AEGIS.
  3. 3AEGIS confirms the payment has arrived.
  4. 4The tenant confirms the agreement has been signed.
  5. 5AEGIS releases the payment to the payout account the lister has confirmed.

AEGIS does not release escrowed money before the release conditions are met, unless a dispute decision or a legal requirement says otherwise. If a deal is cancelled, or a dispute is decided in the tenant's favour, money is returned as decided.

Rentals and sales
4% of the rent or price: 2.3% paid by the tenant and 1.7% paid by whoever listed the property (agency, agent or landlord).
Shortlets
5% in total: 3% paid by the tenant and 2% paid by whoever listed the property.
What it is based on
The rent or price only. It is not charged on the lister's other fees.
Other fees
Fees set by the lister, such as caution, agreement, service charge or an agent fee, are shown itemised and appear on receipts.

We show you the exact amount before you pay. We may change our fees with notice, and a change applies only to new deals.

Payouts: agents, agencies and landlords save their bank details in their profile. For each deal, the lister confirms whether AEGIS should pay that saved account or a different one, such as the property owner, a lawyer or a caretaker. AEGIS pays only the account that was confirmed, so you are responsible for making sure the details are correct.

Both sides can download a receipt for each completed payment.

Pay only through AEGIS for deals that started on AEGIS. If you pay cash or transfer money outside the app, escrow protection does not apply and AEGIS cannot recover the money.

  • Subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play and follow their billing and refund rules.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it in your store settings before the renewal date.
  • We may change prices with notice. A change applies from your next period.
  • Some features may be free for a limited time while listings are being added. We will announce when paid plans start.
8Reports, disputes and resolution

Any user can report any other user (tenant, agent, agency or landlord) or a listing. The report form lets you enter the person's username and describe what happened. Add evidence such as screenshots, receipts, photos or videos.

  • AEGIS may open a conversation involving the people concerned and an AEGIS admin so the matter can be sorted out. We may look into a report before telling the person who was reported.
  • For escrow deals, either side can open a dispute. Both sides can show evidence, and AEGIS reviews it and decides where the escrowed money goes.
  • Our decision decides how AEGIS releases the escrowed money. It does not stop you from going to court or to the authorities.
  • A person who loses a dispute, or has a report upheld against them, loses trust score points. Repeated or serious breaches can lead to suspension.
  • Do not make false or malicious reports. Doing so can lead to action against your account.

Need help with a report or dispute? Email support@aegisproptech.com or use Help & Support in the app.
